🗂️ProjectSequencing.seq — EP · Mixtape · Album

Project Sequencing: got a bunch of songs but not sure what to drop? Send me all of them. I listen to every one and figure out which songs belong on your project — an EP, mixtape or album — and the best order to put them in (that order is called the sequence).

How It Works
EP
Mixtape
Album
  1. Pick your project type. EP, mixtape or album — each one has its own track count and what I need from you (see the tabs above).
  2. Send your audition pool. That's just all the songs you want me to choose from — up to 10 for an EP, 15 for a mixtape, 30 for an album. You upload them through the Client Portal.
  3. I listen to everything. I decide which songs make the project, which ones wait for the next one, and how they flow together.
  4. You get your final tracklist. The songs that made it, in order, with their lengths — plus where any sequence breaks go (short non-song pieces like skits, intros and interludes), the total length, and why I put it in that order.
